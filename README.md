# Manufacturing-Efficiency-Quality-Insights-Using-Python

🏭 Manufacturing Production Analytics – Prime Manufacturing Ltd.

🔎 Overview
This project analyzes one year of production data across multiple factories and lines at Prime Manufacturing Ltd. The goal was to uncover efficiency gaps, downtime hotspots, quality risks, and cost inefficiencies, and provide actionable recommendations for management.

📂 Dataset & Methodology
•	12 months of production records (Jan–Dec 2025)
•	1,000 runs across 3 factories, 4 lines, 40 machines, 100 operators, 5 product categories
•	Cleaned and validated in Python (missing values, outliers, logical ranges, text standardization)
•	Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), aggregation, and visualization performed

📈 Key Findings
*	Strong baseline: 93.4% efficiency, 3.94% defect rate
*	Uneven performance: ~3-point efficiency gap between best and worst lines
*	Downtime paradox: Factory B’s morning shift is the biggest downtime hotspot despite being the most efficient factory overall
*	Quality risk: Washing Machines show highest defect rate (4.31%) and lowest quality score (79.5)
*	Maintenance inefficiency: Semi-Automatic machines cost the most to maintain yet have the highest downtime
*	Energy waste: Energy use shows little correlation with output, suggesting inefficiency

✅ Recommendations
1.	Standardize Factory B’s best practices across all factories
2.	Target Factory C – Line B for immediate process review
3.	Fix Factory B’s morning-shift downtime hotspot
4.	Launch a Washing Machine quality root-cause review
5.	Move Semi-Automatic machines to preventive maintenance
6.	Audit energy usage independent of production volume

📌 Conclusion

Prime Manufacturing Ltd. operates from a position of strength but faces clear opportunities to reduce downtime, improve quality, optimize maintenance, and cut energy waste. Implementing these recommendations will unlock capacity, lower costs, and strengthen competitiveness without major capital investment.
